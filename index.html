Sri Lankan Air Line

SriLankan Airlines, the national flag carrier of Sri Lanka, was originally established in 1979 as Air Lanka before being rebranded with its iconic peacock logo in 1998. Operating from its primary hub at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake, the airline features an all-Airbus fleet that includes long-haul A330 wide-body jets and short-haul A320/A321 narrow-body aircraft. As a proud member of the prestigious oneworld Alliance since 2014, it connects the island nation to an extensive network of over 110 global destinations, offering major direct routes across India, the Maldives, the Middle East, East Asia, Australia, and the United Kingdom. Celebrated worldwide for its signature warm island hospitality, the airline provides a choice between Business and Economy classes, serving authentic Sri Lankan cuisine alongside premium Ceylon tea, while managing its own frequent flyer program, FlySmiLes, to reward loyal passengers.
